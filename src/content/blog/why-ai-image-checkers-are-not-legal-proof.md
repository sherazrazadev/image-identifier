Image Identifier and similar free tools are useful for everyday caution. They are **not** legal proof, forensic certification, or a substitute for professional investigation.

## What “not legal proof” means

- Not a court exhibit by itself.
- Not a lawyer’s opinion or expert witness report.
- Not enough alone for school discipline, HR action, insurance claims, or news publication.
- Not a certificate that an image is authentic or forged.

## Why automated checks fall short for high stakes

Detectors can err both ways. Files change when people screenshot, compress, or repost them. New generators appear faster than public tools update. A single score or label cannot carry the weight of due process.
