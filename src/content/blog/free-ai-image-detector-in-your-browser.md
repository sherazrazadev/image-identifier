Many “AI image detectors” ask you to upload a photo to a remote service. Image Identifier is different: the check runs in your browser on the file you select, so your image is not uploaded to our servers for analysis.

## Why browser-based helps

- You keep the file on your device during the check.
- No account is required.
- The answer stays simple: AI-generated, real, or unclear.

## What still uses the network

The website still loads normal assets (pages, scripts, styles, and runtime files). That is not the same as uploading your photo to a detector API. Details are on the [Privacy](/privacy/) page.

## Try it

Go to [Image Identifier](/), drop a JPEG or PNG, and read the result. First run may take longer while your browser caches runtime files.
