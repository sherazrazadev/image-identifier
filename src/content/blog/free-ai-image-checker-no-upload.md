Searching for a **free AI image checker** usually means one of two things: you want a quick answer, and you do not want to hand your photo to a random website. Image Identifier is built for that.

Drop a JPEG or PNG into the [free tool](/). You get a clear result — **Likely AI-generated**, **Likely real**, or **Unclear**. The check runs in your browser. Your selected image is **not uploaded to our servers** for analysis.

## Why “no upload” matters

Many online detectors ask you to upload a file to their cloud. That can be fine for a public meme. It is less fine for:

- Family photos
- ID-style or workplace images
- Client work you are not allowed to share
- Anything you would not post publicly

A browser-based check keeps the file on your device during the analysis step. You still load normal website assets (pages, scripts, runtime files) over the network — that is not the same as sending your photo to a detector API. See [Privacy](/privacy/) for the plain-language details.

## What the result means (and does not mean)

| Result | Everyday meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Likely AI-generated | Treat the image with extra caution before you trust or share it. |
| Likely real | It looks more like a real photo — still verify important claims elsewhere. |
| Unclear | Not enough signal. Try another file or another source. |

This is **not legal proof**, not a certificate, and not a forensic report. Courts, schools, HR, insurers, and newsrooms need proper process — not a free web label. Read [why AI image checkers are not legal proof](/blog/why-ai-image-checkers-are-not-legal-proof/) and the [FAQ](/faq/).

## Tips for better checks

- Prefer the original file when you can, not a heavy screenshot of a screenshot.
- Very small or heavily compressed images are harder for any checker.
- One result is a hint. Pair it with common sense and trusted sources when the stakes are high.
