People often assume they can “just tell” AI images by looking. Sometimes that works. Often it does not. Modern generators produce hands, text, and skin that look close enough for a quick scroll.

## Myth: weird hands always mean AI

Extra fingers were a popular tell. Newer models make fewer of those mistakes. Relying on one visual quirk alone is outdated.

## Myth: real cameras always leave obvious clues

Many real photos shared online are cropped, filtered, or re-saved. Everyday sharing can strip context. Missing “camera vibes” does not prove a picture is fake.

## Myth: one tool can certify authenticity

No free checker — including ours — can certify that a file is authentic forever. Results are estimates. Conflicting signals happen. That is normal.

## A better habit

- Pause before trusting viral images.
- Run a [quick AI vs real check](/) when something feels off.
- Cross-check the claim with reputable sources when it matters.
- Remember: estimates help; they do not replace judgment.
