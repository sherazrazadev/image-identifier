Searching “is this image AI-generated?” is common now. Ads, profile photos, news screenshots, and product shots can all be synthetic. A free check will not settle every case, but it can give you a quick second opinion before you share, buy, or trust a picture.

## Start with a simple question

Ask only: does this look **AI-generated** or **real**? You do not need a long forensic report for everyday decisions. Image Identifier is built for that short answer.

## How to run a free check

1. Open the [Image Identifier tool](/).
2. Drop a JPEG or PNG (up to 15 MB), or browse for a file.
3. Wait for the result: Likely AI-generated, Likely real, or Unclear.
4. Read the disclaimer — estimates can be wrong.

Prefer a [checker that does not upload your image to our servers](/blog/free-ai-image-checker-no-upload/) when privacy matters.

## What to do with the answer

If the tool says AI-generated, treat the image with extra caution. If it says real, stay curious — edited or re-uploaded photos can still mislead. If it says unclear, look for another source or ask the person who shared the file. Screenshots and social forwards are especially hard — see [checking a screenshot or social photo](/blog/check-screenshot-or-social-photo-for-ai/).

## Limits to keep in mind

Online checkers are helpful, not perfect. Heavy compression, screenshots, and new creative tools can confuse any detector. For school, work, legal, or newsroom decisions, use human review and proper process — not this page alone.
