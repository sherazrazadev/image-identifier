## 5. Key Quality Rules (AGENTS.md & SEO Policy)
1. **Never make false claims:** Keep labels honest ("Likely AI", "Likely real", "Unclear"). Never claim 100% mathematical certainty.
2. **Always link to the core tool:** Every blog post must give readers an immediate reason to test their image on `/`.
3. **No thin keyword clones:** Every post must address a genuinely unique user scenario.
4. **Preserve privacy guarantee:** The core tool must remain client-side with no image bytes uploaded to any backend.
